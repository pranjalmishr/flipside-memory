    YOUR TWO-MINUTE BRAIN BREAK

    Little flips. Big wins.

    1. 1
      Choose your challenge

      Pick Easy, Medium or Hard and press Let’s play.

    2. 2
      Flip two cards

      Find matching pictures. Matches stay face up; different pictures flip back after one second.

    3. 3
      Collect every pair

      Earn 100 points per pair. Each two-card attempt is one move. Your first flip or power-up starts the timer; the final match stops it.

    Make it your vibe: choose Animals, Emojis, Fruits, Space, Nature, or Technology. Collection changes apply to the next game; card backs, board colors, and appearance change instantly without resetting play. Preferences are saved on this browser when storage is available.

    A little help, when you need it

    3 hints: briefly reveal an unmatched pair for 1.5 seconds. 2 shuffles: move only unmatched cards. Finish any open turn first. Power-ups start the timer, never award points or moves, and mark the result Assisted.

    Pause / Resume: hides the board and freezes the timer and flip-back delays. Leaving the tab pauses automatically; returning never resumes without you.

    Autosave: reloads reopen your saved game paused, without counting time away. Matched cards, order, moves, time, and remaining power-ups are restored; unfinished reveals close. Completed games appear in My leaderboard.

    Make every match count ★

    Accuracy = matched pairs ÷ completed two-card moves × 100. Before your first move, accuracy is shown as —.

    Speed bonus: 5 points per full second saved. Beat 01:00 on Easy, 01:30 on Medium, or 03:00 on Hard. The bonus is added once, when you finish.

    • ★★★ At least 80% accuracy, within the level’s target time.
    • ★★ At least 50% accuracy, within twice the target time.
    • ★ Every other completed game. Finishing is a win!

    Keyboard friendly: Tab to a card, use the arrow keys to move, and Enter or Space to flip. P pauses/resumes, H uses a hint, and S shuffles outside forms and dialogs. Escape closes this window.
